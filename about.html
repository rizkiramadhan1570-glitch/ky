About Me

My identity, philosophy, and personal commitment to early childhood development.

Who I Am

Hello! I am Atiah Zainoor Rahma, an undergraduate student majoring in Early Childhood Education (Pendidikan Guru Pendidikan Anak Usia Dini / PGPAUD) at the Faculty of Education, Universitas Negeri Medan (UNIMED).

My journey into early childhood education is guided by a strong belief that the golden age of a child—the first five to eight years—is the most foundational phase in human development. During these years, emotional safety, creative stimulation, curiosity, and love build the neural pathways that determine how a person learns, cares, and thrives for a lifetime.

Guided by my motto, “Every experience is a chance to learn, grow, and become a better version of myself,” I continually challenge myself to learn modern pedagogical methods, incorporate digital literacy thoughtfully into early classrooms, and foster an environment where every child feels cherished, heard, and empowered.

Core Educational Philosophy

Principle 1

Play-Based Learning

Play is children's natural work. Through imaginative and structured play, children explore the world, cultivate problem-solving abilities, and develop socio-emotional resilience.

Principle 2

Child-Centered Empathy

Every child is unique with distinct learning paces and emotional temperaments. Observing each child patiently allows educators to scaffold their individual potentials without pressure.

Principle 3

Balanced Digital Literacy

In our modern era, technology should enrich early learning as an interactive, storytelling, and creative medium while prioritizing screen-time balance, sensory engagement, and real-world social interaction.

Principle 4

Collaborative Community

Effective early childhood education is a warm partnership between teachers, families, and communities to establish safe and nurturing environments.
