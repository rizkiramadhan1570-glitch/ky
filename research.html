Undergraduate Research

1. Observational Study: Play-Based Social Skills Development in Kindergarten

Focus: Investigated how loose-parts play and thematic roleplay areas influence empathy, sharing, and verbal negotiation among children aged 4-6 in Medan.

Methodology: Qualitative observation protocols & descriptive field notes.

My Contribution: Formulated observation rubrics, gathered daily behavioral data across 4 weeks, and authored the analytical synthesis report.

Educational Technology

2. Interactive Early Literacy Web & Visual Sitemap Architecture

Focus: Conceptualized and developed a complete visual sitemap (via draw.io) and multi-page web platform tailored for parents and teachers of young children.

Tools: Draw.io (Diagrams.net), HTML5, CSS3

My Contribution: 100% Individual task for Digital Literacy P10 (Hierarchical tree structure, semantic web layout, and accessible responsive styling).

Collaborative Project

3. "Cerita Ceria" - Digital Illustrated Storybook for Emotional Literacy

Focus: Collaborative creation of an interactive digital flipbook helping preschool children identify and express fundamental emotions (joy, sadness, anger, fear).

Tools: Canva for Education, FlipHTML5, Audio Recording

My Contribution: Lead author for story scripts, character dialogues, and recorded educational voiceover narration.

Instructional Media

4. Sensory Nature Exploration Kit (Loose Parts Learning)

Focus: Developed a hands-on learning kit utilizing natural elements (twigs, seeds, shells, smooth river stones) to stimulate tactile sensory integration and early classification skills.

Target Audience: Kindergarten Group A (Ages 4-5)

My Contribution: Curated safe natural specimens, drafted educator guidebooks, and piloted the kit in a partner preschool.
